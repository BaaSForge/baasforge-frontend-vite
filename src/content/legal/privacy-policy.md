# BaaSForge Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 | Last updated: July 23, 2026**

BaaSForge is a backend architecture platform that generates, deploys, and monitors backend infrastructure from natural language prompts. It is operated by BForge Lab ("BForge Lab," "we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

This policy applies to baasforge.com, the BaaSForge console, our CLI, our API, and related services (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described here.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the "EU/UK Supplement" section below applies to you in addition to the general terms.

---

## 1. Who we are and our role

BaaSForge acts in two capacities:

**Controller.** For account data, billing data, and usage data collected to operate and improve the Services, BaaSForge is the data controller.

**Processor.** For content you submit through prompts, and for any data that flows through the backends you generate and deploy (databases, API payloads, logs from your deployed infrastructure), BaaSForge acts as a processor on your behalf. You remain the controller of that data. A Data Processing Addendum (DPA), incorporating the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, is available to customers on request and governs our processor obligations.

Contact for privacy matters: **info@baasforge.com**

---

## 2. Data we collect

**Account information.** Name, email address, password (hashed), company name, billing address, payment details (processed by our payment processor, not stored by us).

**Prompts and generated content.** The natural language prompts you submit to build backend units (API, Workflow, Queue Worker, Actor), the specifications and code BaaSForge generates in response, and your edits to that output.

**Deployment and infrastructure data.** Configuration, logs, metrics, and traces generated by backends you deploy through BaaSForge, to the extent needed to run, monitor, and debug your infrastructure.

**Usage data.** Pages visited, features used, session duration, referring URLs, device and browser type, IP address, and approximate location derived from IP.

**Communications.** Support tickets, emails, community messages, and survey responses you send us.

**Cookies and similar technologies.** Session cookies (required for login), analytics cookies, and, where applicable, marketing cookies. See Section 9.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of data (health, biometric, religious, political, etc.) and ask that you do not submit such data in prompts or deployed workloads unless a data processing agreement specifically covers it.

## 4. AI model training and prompt use

BaaSForge uses non-reasoning, cost-efficient models tuned to our proprietary framework to translate prompts into backend infrastructure. Regarding your prompts and generated output:

- We do **not** use customer prompts or generated code to train models shared across customers, by default.
- We do **not** sell prompts, generated code, or deployment data to third parties, under any circumstance.
- If we ever introduce an opt-in program where anonymized or aggregated prompt data helps improve BaaSForge's models, it will be off by default and clearly disclosed, consistent with data minimization principles under GDPR Article 5.
- Enterprise and EU customers may request a data processing agreement that contractually excludes their data from any improvement program entirely.

You are told, at the point of use, that BaaSForge output is AI-generated. This disclosure exists in the product interface and in generated code artifacts (for example, via a header comment or manifest tag), consistent with the transparency obligations in Article 50 of the EU AI Act for AI systems that generate content. Because BaaSForge produces infrastructure code for professional developer use rather than synthetic media (audio, image, video, or text content aimed at the public), it does not fall within the AI Act's high-risk categories under Annex III, but we apply the transparency standard voluntarily as good practice.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure

We share data only in the following circumstances:

- **Infrastructure and sub-processors.** Cloud hosting, database, logging, email delivery, payment processing, and analytics providers who process data on our behalf under contract. A current sub-processor list is available at **[baasforge.com/subprocessors]**.
- **Deployment targets.** If you configure BaaSForge to deploy to your own cloud account or infrastructure, data flows there under your control, not ours.
- **Business transfers.** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice to you.
- **Legal requirements.** Where required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, and to protect the rights, property, or safety of BaaSForge, our users, or others.
- **With your direction.** Any other sharing you explicitly authorize (for example, connecting a third-party integration).

We do not share personal data with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

## 6. International data transfers

BaaSForge is a US-based company at launch, expanding to EU and MENA infrastructure over time. Where personal data is transferred from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to the United States or another country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, where applicable), together with supplementary technical and organizational measures.

As we open EU-region infrastructure, EU customers will be able to opt to keep prompt, deployment, and generated-code data within EU data centers. This is planned but not yet available; this policy will be updated when it ships.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict or object to processing
- Port your data to another provider
- Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact **info@baasforge.com**. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (30 days under GDPR, extendable by 60 days for complex requests).

Because prompts and generated code may be embedded in deployed backend infrastructure, deletion requests involving generated code will remove it from BaaSForge-managed storage; infrastructure already deployed to your own cloud account is outside our control and must be managed by you.

## 10. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, least-privilege infrastructure access, and regular security review. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and, where required, supervisory authorities without undue delay in the event of a personal data breach, consistent with GDPR Article 33/34.

## 11. Children

The Services are not directed to individuals under 16, and in the United States, not to children under 13 within the meaning of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 13. US State Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law in effect, you may have additional rights, including:

- The right to know what personal data we collect and why
- The right to delete personal data we hold about you
- The right to correct inaccurate personal data
- The right to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal data (including for cross-context behavioral advertising), and out of certain profiling
- The right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights

**Sale and sharing.** BaaSForge does not sell personal data for money. If our use of analytics or marketing cookies is considered "sharing" under California law because it involves cross-context behavioral advertising, you can opt out via [Cookie settings](/cookie-settings) or by sending a recognized opt-out preference signal (such as Global Privacy Control), which we will honor for browsers where it is detectable.

**Sensitive personal information.** We do not use sensitive personal information (as defined under CPRA) for purposes beyond what is necessary to provide the Services.

**Financial incentives.** We do not offer financial incentives in exchange for personal data.

To exercise these rights, contact **info@baasforge.com**. We will verify your identity before fulfilling access, deletion, or correction requests, and will respond within the timeframe required by the applicable state law.
